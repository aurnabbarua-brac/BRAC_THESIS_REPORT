\section*{Ethics Statement}

This study focuses on the development and evaluation of deep learning models for skin condition classification and severity estimation using image datasets. The research relies on image data for computational analysis and does not involve direct clinical examinations, medical interventions, or experiments on human participants conducted by the researcher. The datasets are used for academic and research purposes, subject to their respective access conditions and terms of use. Appropriate care is taken to acknowledge the original data sources and cite relevant publications.\\

The study aims to support the interpretation of model predictions through Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM). However, the generated predictions and visual explanations are intended for research purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or clinical judgment. The limitations of the models and the possibility of incorrect predictions are acknowledged. The research does not provide treatment recommendations or claim clinical validity.\\


All reported results are presented to reflect the models' observed performance on the selected datasets. The study does not intentionally manipulate evaluation results or misrepresent model capabilities. Any future work involving patient recruitment, identifiable medical images, clinical validation, or deployment in a healthcare setting will require appropriate ethical review, permissions, and safeguards before implementation.
